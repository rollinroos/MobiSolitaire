## Promotional text
Big numbers, one bold symbol and no clutter. Switch between Large Print, Classic and Glass cards any time, even in the middle of a game.

## Description
Klondike solitaire with cards you can actually read.

MobiSolitaire has a Large Print deck with oversized numbers and one big suit symbol on every card, in strong black and red on plain white. When cards overlap, the numbers stay in view. The move counter and timer are big too.

Prefer a traditional look? Switch decks with one tap, even in the middle of a game.

THREE CARD STYLES
• Large Print: big numbers, one symbol, high contrast
• Classic: traditional cards with illustrated kings, queens and jacks
• Glass: a bright, modern take on the classic deck

THREE TABLE LAYOUTS
• Deck at the top left
• Deck at the top right
• Deck and foundations at the bottom, closer to your thumb

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Draw 1 or Draw 3
• Tap a card to move it, or drag it where you want it
• Undo up to 100 moves
• Finish the game automatically once the deck is empty and every card is face up
• Your game is saved, so you can pick up where you left off

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
• No ads
• No in-app purchases
• No account
• Works offline
• Collects no data

## Notes for App Review
MobiSolitaire is a single-player Klondike solitaire game. It needs no sign-in, has no purchases and makes no network connections. The round button at the top left changes the table layout. The round button at the top right changes the card style.
