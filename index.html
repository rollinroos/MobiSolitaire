Support

MobiSolitaire is a Klondike solitaire game for iPhone with a large print deck.

Need help?

Email mobimeeds@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I change the cards?

Tap the round button at the top right. It switches between Classic, Glass and Large Print. You can change decks in the middle of a game.

How do I move the deck?

Tap the round button at the top left. It switches between the deck at the top left, at the top right, and at the bottom.

How do I move a card?

Tap a card to send it to a place it can go, or drag it where you want it.

Can I draw three cards?

Yes. Tap the Draw button at the bottom and choose Draw 1 or Draw 3. Changing it starts a new game.

Can I undo a move?

Yes. Tap the curved arrow at the bottom. You can undo up to 100 moves.

Is my game saved?

Yes, on your iPhone, after every move. Deleting the app deletes your saved game.

What does it need?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. It works without an internet connection.
